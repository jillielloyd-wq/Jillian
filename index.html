Cherry Blossoms

A Brief History

Cherry blossoms, known as Sakura in Japan, have been celebrated for centuries. They represent a time of renewal and the fleeting nature of life. In 1912, Japan gifted over 3,000 trees to the United States as a symbol of friendship, which is why we see so many beautiful blooms in Washington D.C. every spring!
